08 / ANDROID BLUETOOTH PHOTO + QR TICKETS

One dedicated ticket printer.

Physical print test over explicitly paired secure Bluetooth SPP: 384-dot monochrome guest snapshot plus a native ESC/POS QR. Supports QR-only fallback. Most 58mm ESC/POS printers use 384 dots; printer command compatibility, paper and print quality are NOT yet hardware-verified. No cutter / logo / Chinese commands.

Checking Android printer bridge...

For a physical photo test, first apply the Camera in 02 / DEVICE CHECK. The TEST ticket uses the current camera image and a deliberately NON-WORKING diagnostic QR; a test is never a real guest video link. Actual tickets use the configured countdown still (default T-2). If the ticket contains a photo, screen QR remains visible even after Bluetooth writes.

Pair in Android Settings first. The app lists paired devices only; it does not discover or silently choose same-name devices. Automatic printing also needs the QR & Auto Return print route enabled. Connection and automatic permission reset when the app pauses.

Print or intentionally reprint a saved QR

New recordings with photo receipts print their countdown still + private QR. Legacy recordings can use their previously saved still (not the countdown moment). QR-only receipts print a QR. Only the selected recording is printed. Its URL is never replaced by the newest guest or active event. Diagnostic QR is not a video link. The guest QR includes owner/delete access: keep the paper private.

Advanced: accept fast return without a paper sensor

Default: keep the screen QR after sending. Fast return is an explicit risk acceptance, not confirmation that every ticket printed. Print a real guest QR, scan it with a phone, and paste its COMPLETE unchanged URL below. No URL is logged or sent to analytics.

A native confirmation is required. Acceptance lasts at most four hours and only for this connection, origin and tested URL length. Disconnect, pause or uncertain output clears it. Longer links retain the screen QR.
