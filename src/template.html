04 / RECEIPT DESIGN

Your paper keepsake.

These fields are saved with the Event & Capture settings. Every captured message keeps its own receipt design snapshot.

The image is captured from the LIVE camera canvas during the 3-second countdown, before recording begins. If countdown is disabled, the still is taken at recording start. Snapshot is retained locally for this ticket.

58mm ESC/POS test: photo uses a 384-dot monochrome image plus a native QR command. Paper output and logo/layout matching are device-dependent; 80mm printers may center the smaller photo. No cutter command.
